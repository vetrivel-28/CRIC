How to read these scores

Scores are relative to the eligible bowlers in this request, not probabilities. Each metric is normalized against that set, then evidence is shrunk toward neutral according to its sample size. Available metrics and evidence sources are reweighted; missing evidence is omitted, not scored as poor performance.
